When a beginner gets a green signal, their first impulse is often to click "100% All-In" on a single market order.

This is the fastest route to psychological pain. If price drops even 3%, fear strikes, and they panic sell right at the bottom.

## The 4-Bucket Split-Entry Ladder

Instead of going all-in, we split our spot trading capital into four deliberate buckets:

1. **Bucket 1 (~13% - Test Entry):** Place a small <Term client:visible id="limit-order">Limit Order</Term> at Support Floor 1. If price bounces immediately, you participate in the rally. If it drops, you have barely touched your capital.
2. **Bucket 2 (~21% - Deeper Value):** Place a resting limit order at Support Floor 2. You get cheaper coins automatically.
3. **Bucket 3 (~30% - Maximum Discount):** Place a resting limit order at Support Floor 3 (lowest major support).
4. **Bucket 4 (~36% - Untouched Cash Reserve):** This cash stays in your USDT wallet. You **never** spend it on this trade.

## The Peace of Mind Rule

Notice what happens after your first purchase: **you still hold 87% of your capital safely in cash**!

Even if all three entry tiers fill during a volatile flush, you still have **36% untouched cash**, and your stop-loss sits 2% below your lowest floor. One bad trade can never wipe you out.

## Real-Life Spot Example: Your First `$50` Gate.com SOL Plan

Instead of buying `$50` worth of SOL in one click:

1. **Entry 1 (`$6.42`, ~13%):** Spot Limit Buy at `$88.50` (`0.0725 SOL`, well over Gate.com's `1 USDT` minimum).
2. **Entry 2 (`$10.70`, ~21%):** Optional deeper Spot Limit Buy at `$85.00`.
3. **Entry 3 (`$14.97`, ~30%):** Optional final floor Spot Limit Buy at `$81.20`.
4. **Reserve (`$17.91`, ~36%):** Stays untouched in USDT. Once Entry 1 fills, you immediately place an automatic Spot TP/SL Stop at **`$79.58`** and profit target near **`$95.00`** (`+7.3%` gain vs `~0.4%` round-trip fee).
