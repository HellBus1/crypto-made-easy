## Step 1: Make Sure You Are on the "Spot" Tab

Before you look at any chart, check the top menu of your exchange and make sure you clicked <Term client:visible id="spot-market">Spot Market</Term> (never _Futures_, _Perpetuals_, or _Margin_).

1. **Spot trading** means buying the real coin directly with your own <Term client:visible id="usdt">USDT</Term> digital dollar cash.
2. If you buy **1 SOL for $128.18** on Spot, you own **1 SOL**. Even if the price drops 20% next week, the exchange can never force-liquidate you or take your coin away.
3. **Futures / Margin** uses borrowed money and can wipe out your account to zero in minutes. Beginners should stay **100% on Spot**.

## Step 3: How Time (X-Axis) and Price (Y-Axis) Build a Chart

Every crypto chart is just a simple two-direction grid:

```
Price (USDT)  ▲
   $135.00 ───┼───────────────────────────────────────  ◄── Resistance Ceiling (Where sellers cashed out)
              │          ┌─┐
   $130.00 ───┼──────────│ │────── Smooth MA30 Line ──  ◄── 30-Bar Fair-Price Average ($129.07)
              │   ┌─┐    └─┘    ┌─┐
   $128.18 ───┼───│ │───────────│ │───────────────────  ◄── Current Price ($128.18 - On Discount!)
              │   └─┘           └┬┘
   $126.64 ───┼──────────────────┴────────────────────  ◄── Support Floor (Where buyers bounced before)
              └──────────┬──────────────┬────────────►
                    Yesterday         Today             Time (4H or 1D Bars)
```

- **Bottom Edge (Left to Right = Time):** Moving right means moving forward in time. Each vertical bar is one <Term client:visible id="candlestick">Candlestick</Term> covering your chosen <Term client:visible id="timeframe">Timeframe (4H or 1D)</Term>.
- **Right Edge (Bottom to Top = Price in USDT):** Moving up means the coin is more expensive in <Term client:visible id="usdt">USDT</Term>; moving down means the coin is cheaper.

## Quick Beginner Checklist Before Moving to Chapter 2

- **Am I on the Spot tab?** Yes, buying with my own USDT cash.
- **Did I switch the timeframe button above the chart to `4H` or `1D`?** Yes, so each bar represents 4 hours or 1 day.
- **Can I spot the 5 key numbers?** Current Price, <Term client:visible id="ma30">MA30</Term>, <Term client:visible id="rsi">RSI</Term>, <Term client:visible id="order-book">Order Book Buyer %</Term>, and the nearest <Term client:visible id="support">Support Floor</Term>.
