When you open a crypto exchange like **Gate.com** or **Binance** for the very first time, the screen looks like an airplane cockpit. Numbers flash green and red every second, lines zig-zag across a grid, and **dozens of tiny buttons** (`1m`, `MA`, `BOLL`, `RSI`, `SRL`, `VPVR`, `SAR`, `SKDJ`) compete for your attention.

If you felt confused, **nothing is wrong with you**. Exchange screens pack tools for high-speed day traders, futures gamblers, and calm spot buyers onto a single page.

## Step 2: The Dozens of Tiny Buttons (`1m`, `MA`, `BOLL`, `RSI`) -> Only Use What Is Essential First!

Look at the bottom edge of the Gate.com chart in the screenshot above. You will see a long row of 17 tiny buttons:

`SRL` · `Turnover` · **`MA`** · `EMA` · **`Volume`** · `MACD` · **`BOLL`** · `VPVR` · `SAR` · `AVL` · `MTM` · `VR` · `DMA` · `SKDJ` · `PSY` · `BBI` · `>` (**`RSI`**)

**Do not turn all of them on!** If you click every button, your chart turns into unreadable rainbow spaghetti. To jump into market reading and spot trading as a beginner, you only need **4 essential controls**:

1. **Top-Left Timeframe Row -> Click `4h` or `1D` (Skip `1m`):** Never trade on `1m` (1-minute), where random noise makes every candle look like an emergency. Click `4h` (4-hour bars) or `1D` (daily bars).
2. **Bottom Bar -> Click `MA` (<Term client:visible id="ma30">Moving Average</Term>):** Draws one smooth fair-price line across the candles so you immediately see if the coin is **on discount** (Light 1).
3. **Bottom Bar -> Click `BOLL` (<Term client:visible id="bollinger-bands">Bollinger Bands</Term>):** Wraps a visual **Upper Roof** and **Lower Floor** tunnel around the candles so you never buy at the top.
4. **Bottom Bar -> Click `>` then `RSI` (<Term client:visible id="rsi">Exhaustion Meter</Term>):** Opens a `0 to 100` speedometer at the bottom. When **RSI is 36 or lower**, sellers are tired (Light 2).

## Step 3: Beginner Cheat-Sheet (What to Use Now vs What to Ignore)

| Button on Gate.com                                                    | Where It Lives on Screen                     | Beginner Status                 | Plain-English Job                                                                    |
| :-------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------ | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **`4h` / `1D`**                                                       | Top-left row above chart (`1m 15m 1h 4h 1D`) | **ESSENTIAL (Click First)**     | Zooms out so 1 candle bar = 4 hours or 1 full day. Filters out 1-minute panic noise. |
| **`MA` (`MA30`)**                                                     | Bottom bar, 3rd button from left             | **ESSENTIAL (Light 1)**         | Smooth 30-bar average price line. Candles below `MA30` = coin is on sale.            |
| **`BOLL`**                                                            | Bottom bar, 7th button from left             | **ESSENTIAL (Floor/Roof)**      | Draws the Upper Roof (`UB`) and Lower Bounce Floor (`LB`) around the candles.        |
| **`RSI`**                                                             | Bottom bar, click `>` arrow on right         | **ESSENTIAL (Light 2)**         | `0–100` exhaustion meter. `36` or lower = sellers are tired. `70+` = overheated.     |
| **`Volume`**                                                          | Bottom bar, 5th button (On by default)       | **ESSENTIAL (Context)**         | Shows crowd size underneath each candle (`Volume 14.51` in screenshot).              |
| **`Order Book`**                                                      | Middle column (`Red Sellers / Green Buyers`) | **ESSENTIAL (Light 3)**         | Shows real waiting buy orders. We want at least `40%` Green Buyers before buying.    |
| `1m` / `15m`                                                          | Top-left row above chart                     | **Ignore (Too Noisy)**          | Changes every 60 seconds; causes emotional over-trading.                             |
| `SRL`, `VPVR`, `SAR`, `AVL`, `MTM`, `VR`, `DMA`, `SKDJ`, `PSY`, `BBI` | Bottom indicator strip                       | **Ignore (Day-Trader Clutter)** | Redundant or hyper-fast math formulas that clutter the chart for beginners.          |

## Step 4: How Time (X-Axis) and Price (Y-Axis) Build the Chart Grid

Once you have switched to **`4h`** or **`1D`** and turned on **`MA`** and **`BOLL`**, the chart is just a calm two-direction map:

```
Price (USDT)  ▲
   $80,500 ───┼───────────────────────────────────────  ◄── Resistance Ceiling / BOLL Upper Roof
              │          ┌─┐
   $76,410 ───┼──────────│ │────── Smooth MA30 Line ──  ◄── 30-Bar Fair-Price Average
              │   ┌─┐    └─┘    ┌─┐
   $75,925 ───┼───│ │───────────│ │───────────────────  ◄── Current Price (Below MA30 = On Discount!)
              │   └─┘           └┬┘
   $75,500 ───┼──────────────────┴────────────────────  ◄── Support Floor / BOLL Lower Band
              └──────────┬──────────────┬────────────►
                    Yesterday         Today             Time (4H or 1D Bars)
```

- **Bottom Edge (Left to Right = Time):** Moving right means moving forward in time. Each vertical bar is one <Term client:visible id="candlestick">Candlestick</Term> covering 4 hours (`4h`) or 1 day (`1D`).
- **Right Edge (Bottom to Top = Price in USDT):** Moving up means the coin costs more <Term client:visible id="usdt">USDT</Term>; moving down means the coin is on discount.
