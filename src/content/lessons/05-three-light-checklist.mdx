The core philosophy of **Crypto Made Easy** is simple: **we never guess, and we never buy because of fear of missing out (FOMO)**.

Instead, we treat spot trading like crossing a busy three-lane street. You do not step out into traffic if only Lane 1 is clear while cars are speeding down Lane 2 and 3. You only cross when **all 3 lights are green at the exact same moment**.

## The 3 Lights Breakdown

### Light 1: Is the coin on discount?

- **Condition:** Current Price is below the 30-period Moving Average (<Term client:visible id="ma30">MA30</Term>) or touching the lower Bollinger Band.
- **Meaning:** You are buying below the average cost of recent participants, not paying a premium.

### Light 2: Have sellers exhausted their energy?

- **Condition:** <Term client:visible id="rsi">RSI</Term> is at or below **36**.
- **Meaning:** The momentum spring has been compressed downward. Panic selling is drying up.

### Light 3: Are buyers waiting in the Order Book?

- **Condition:** Green Buyer Bid % is at least **40%** (Ask % &le; 60%).
- **Meaning:** Real buyers have placed resting limit orders below. You are not stepping into thin air.

## Real Case Comparison: SOL vs ETH

In our documented case from November 2026:

- **Solana (SOL):** Price was below MA30 ($89.73 vs $91.60), RSI was 36, and Order Book was 40.61% Buyers. **All 3 lights were ON -> Buy Zone!**
- **Ethereum (ETH):** Price was below MA30 and RSI was 34, **but** Order Book Buyers were only 22% (78% aggressive sellers). **Light 3 remained OFF -> Wait!**

Waiting on ETH protected traders from catching a falling knife.

## Real-Life Spot Example: When 3/3 Lights Still Means "Wait" (BTC Crash Rule)

Suppose `SOL` shows all 3 lights ON (`Price $89.73 < MA30 $91.60`, `RSI 36`, `Bid 40.61%`) **right while Bitcoin drops `-7.2%` in 24 hours**:

- On the Signal Checker, you enter `BTC 24h Change = -7.2` and `1D/4H Structure = Multi-day crash`.
- The checker highlights the **Market Context Caution** warning: altcoins usually follow Bitcoin lower during market-wide flushes.
- Instead of rushing `$6.42` into a falling market, you wait until Bitcoin stabilizes above `-5%` or test the scenario safely in the [Paper-Trade Journal](/journal).
