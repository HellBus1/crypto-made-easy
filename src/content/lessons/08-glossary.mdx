## Real-Life Spot Example: Gate.com Pre-Click Button Check

Right before submitting any order on Gate.com Spot:

1. Check top tab says **Spot** (not Margin `3x` / Futures).
2. Confirm order mode says **Limit** (`$88.50` price + `$6.42` total), never blind **Market**.
3. As soon as filled, open **TP/SL** and lock your automatic **Stop-Loss** (`$79.58`) + **Take-Profit** (`$95.00`).
