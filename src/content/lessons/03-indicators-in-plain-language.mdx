When beginners open Gate.com, they see 17+ indicator buttons along the bottom of the chart (`SRL`, `Turnover`, `MA`, `EMA`, `Volume`, `MACD`, `BOLL`, `VPVR`, `SAR`, `AVL`, `MTM`, `VR`, `DMA`, `SKDJ`, `PSY`, `BBI`, `RSI`).

Here is the secret that experienced spot traders know: **you do not need 17 indicators**. In fact, turning on more than 3 or 4 indicators at the same time makes your chart harder to read, not easier.

## Step 1: The Only 4 Essential Chart Indicators You Need Right Now

1. **`MA` (<Term client:visible id="ma30">MA30 Moving Average</Term> - Light 1):**
   - **What it is:** The average closing price of the last 30 candles drawn as one smooth line.
   - **Why it matters:** If Current Price is **below MA30**, you are buying on discount instead of overpaying at the top.
   - **Rule:** Pick **MA** (or EMA), never both at once.
2. **`BOLL` (<Term client:visible id="bollinger-bands">Bollinger Bands</Term> - Light 1 Helper):**
   - **What it is:** A 3-line tunnel around price showing the **Upper Roof (`UB`)**, **Middle Average (`MB`)**, and **Lower Floor (`LB`)**.
   - **Why it matters:** Shows you at a glance whether price is near the cheap floor (`LB`) or expensive roof (`UB`).
3. **`RSI` (<Term client:visible id="rsi">Relative Strength Index</Term> - Light 2):**
   - **What it is:** A `0 to 100` momentum meter underneath the main chart.
   - **Why it matters:** When **RSI is 36 or lower**, recent selling is exhausted and stretched like a rubber band (Light 2 turns ON). When **RSI is 70 or higher**, buyers are overheated.
4. **`Volume` (<Term client:visible id="volume">Trading Activity Bars</Term> - Context):**
   - **What it is:** Vertical green and red pillars underneath the candles showing how many coins traded during each bar.
   - **Why it matters:** Confirms whether a price move has a real crowd behind it.

## Quick Beginner Checklist Before Moving to Chapter 4

- **Which 4 buttons do I keep ON?** `MA`, `BOLL`, `RSI`, and `Volume` (on a `4h` or `1D` chart).
- **What turns Light 1 ON?** Current Price is below `MA30` (or touching the `BOLL` Lower Band) near a Support Floor.
- **What turns Light 2 ON?** `RSI` is `36` or lower.
